About Us

Who are we?

Askalany and Karim

We are Askalany and Karim, two very dedicated farmers who decided to build a compiler instead of only planting crops. Farmer Compiler turns simple smart-farm rules into structured compiler outputs, proving how source code moves through the full compilation pipeline.

The project combines agriculture, IoT logic, custom grammar, Arabizi-style keywords, and compiler construction concepts in one educational system.
